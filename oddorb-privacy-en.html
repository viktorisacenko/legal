2. In short

  • You don't need an account. We never ask for your name or email address.
  • Your progress and settings stay on your device. We run no servers of our own and never receive this data.
  • The current version of the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash reporting services. Ads and purchases in this test version are placeholders; nothing is charged.

3. Data in the app

Progress and settings

Progress, stars, shards, collection, achievements and settings (e.g. sound, colour-blind mode, handedness) are stored only on your device. You can delete them with “Reset progress” in the app's settings or by uninstalling the app.

Technical device information (Unity)

The app is built with the Unity game engine by Unity Technologies. At start-up the Unity runtime may send technical information about your device to Unity, such as device model, operating system, processor, graphics chip and screen resolution. Unity uses it for statistics about the hardware in use. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in a working engine tuned to common devices (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). More: Unity's privacy policy.

Google Play and the Apple App Store

The app is distributed through Google Play and the Apple App Store. When you download or update it, Google or Apple process data under their own responsibility and privacy policies. If you have allowed your device to share diagnostics, we receive aggregated statistics through the developer consoles, such as install counts and crash and performance reports. They don't identify you.

Permissions

The Android app uses the “vibrate” permission for haptic feedback and the “internet access” permission that comes with the Unity runtime (see above). The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

4. These pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address, date and time and the page requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). They set no cookies and uses no tracking. More: GitHub's privacy statement.

5. Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your email address and message only to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once your request is settled and no retention obligation applies.

6. Transfers outside the EU

Unity, Google, Apple and GitHub may also process data in the USA. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

7. Children

ODD ORB is intended for people aged 13 and over.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Write to support@oddorb.app. You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, e.g. the Bavarian Data Protection Authority (BayLDA), Promenade 18, 91522 Ansbach, Germany.

9. Changes

Before the app uses advertising, analytics or crash reporting services or real purchases, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
